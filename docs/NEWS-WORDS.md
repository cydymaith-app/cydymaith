# News words: collection

Phrases heard in Welsh news bulletins. They are collected per upload and then given one home each, as in `FORECAST-WORDS.md` and `SPORT-WORDS.md`.

Sources to watch: *Newyddion S4C*, Radio Cymru bulletins, and S4C's political programmes. Record phrases only, never a transcript. Every Welsh word stays a draft until a fluent speaker has checked it (`REVIEW.md`).

## The standard

- **Phrases, not words.** The rows are news phrases at weather level: a clause or part-sentence as a newsreader says it, never a bare noun.
- **Key words** (in bold below) are the coloured words. Each one is matched to its English in the other pack.
- **Places only inside a phrase** (rule 7). Examples are *yn Nhŷ'r Cyffredin*, *ym Mrwsel* and *yn y Tŷ Gwyn*. No lists of capitals, constituencies or parliaments.
- **Notes explain the change a place or word goes through**, in the weather style: "{{Tŷ}} becomes {{Nhŷ}} after {{yn}}".
- **Figures are written as spoken.** *5%* is *pump y cant* and *34 oed* is *tri deg pedwar oed*. The caption form is "also heard as".

1. **People and parties.** Proposed: no names of people, as with presenters and storms. Party names only inside a heard phrase.

- **Where the Welsh differs from the caption.** The captions are typed live, in speech spelling (*gorrod*, *Wneiff*, *rŵan*, *dre*, *pobol*, *belly 'de*).
