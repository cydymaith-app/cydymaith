# News words: collection

Phrases heard in Welsh news bulletins. They are collected per upload and then given one home each, as in `FORECAST-WORDS.md` and `SPORT-WORDS.md`.

Sources to watch: *Newyddion S4C*, Radio Cymru bulletins, and S4C's political programmes. Record phrases only, never a transcript. Every Welsh word stays a draft until a fluent speaker has checked it (`REVIEW.md`).

## The standard

- **Phrases, not words.** The rows are news phrases at weather level: a clause or part-sentence as a newsreader says it, never a bare noun.
- **Key words** (in bold below) are the coloured words. Each one is matched to its English in the other pack.
- **Places only inside a phrase** (rule 7). Examples are *yn Nhŷ'r Cyffredin*, *ym Mrwsel* and *yn y Tŷ Gwyn*. No lists of capitals, constituencies or parliaments.
- **Notes explain the change a place or word goes through**, in the weather style: "{{Tŷ}} becomes {{Nhŷ}} after {{yn}}".
- **Figures are written as spoken.** *5%* is *pump y cant* and *34 oed* is *tri deg pedwar oed*. The caption form is "also heard as".

### How it's said

| Welsh | English |
|---|---|
| **Yn ôl** yr heddlu, … | **According to** the police, … |
| **Dywedodd** **llefarydd ar ran** y cwmni … | A **spokesperson for** the firm **said** … |
| **Mewn datganiad**, dywedodd y bwrdd iechyd … | **In a statement**, the health board said … |
| Mae'r cwmni wedi **gwrthod gwneud sylw** | The firm has **declined to comment** |
| **Cyhoeddwyd** y ffigurau heddiw | The figures **were published** today |
| **Mae disgwyl i**'r gweinidog gyhoeddi'r manylion yfory | The minister **is expected to** announce the details tomorrow |
| **Daw hyn wedi i**'r cyngor gau dwy ysgol | **This comes after** the council closed two schools |
| Ein **gohebydd gwleidyddol** sydd yn y Senedd | Our **political correspondent** is at the Senedd |
| **Dyma'r penawdau** | **Here are the headlines** |
| Mwy am hynny **yn y man** | More on that **shortly** |

## To decide

1. **People and parties.** Proposed: no names of people, as with presenters and storms. Party names only inside a heard phrase.
2. **Dated stories.** A heard phrase can tie itself to one event (tariffs, a named steelworks). Keep it as heard, or make it general?
3. **His and her forms.** One row with the other form as "also heard as", plus a note on the mutation.
4. **Page count.** Eight pages is more than sport's five. Possible merges: Emergencies into Crime and courts, and Farming and tourism into Money and work.

### Upload 1: teaching assistants' pay protest (*Newyddion S4C*, 2026-10-29)

Seven caption photos. The story: teaching assistants protesting about pay for the school holidays. Phrases in the order heard (the studio lead-in, then the report).

| Heard | Draft English | Likely home |
|---|---|---|
| …delio â **phwysau cynyddol** i weithio mwy, **heb dderbyn tal ychwanegol** | …dealing with growing pressure to work more, without receiving extra pay | Money and work |
| **Ar hyn o bryd**, dim ond am eu gwaith **yn ystod y tymor** | At present, only for their work during term | How it's said (*Ar hyn o bryd*); Public services |
| …mae **cynorthwywyr dysgu** yn cael eu talu **sef 39 wythnos o'r flwyddyn** | …teaching assistants are paid, that is, 39 weeks of the year | Public services; figures as spoken (*tri deg naw wythnos*) |
| **Bu dwsinau** ohonyn nhw'n **protestio**'r bore 'ma | Dozens of them protested this morning | Politics or Money and work; *Bu* = past of *bod*, used for events |
| …**yn galw am** fwy o **gydnabyddiaeth** | …calling for more recognition | How it's said (*galw am*) |
| **Yn ôl Llywodraeth Cymru**, maen nhw'n **ystyried y sefyllfa** | According to the Welsh Government, they are considering the situation | How it's said (*Yn ôl*, *ystyried*) |
| **Mwy gan** [reporter's name] | More from [reporter] | How it's said (sign-off from a report) |

Notes for synthesis:

- *delio â* and *galw am* are verb plus preposition. *Mwy gan* is a report sign-off; the name is not kept.
- *protestio'r bore 'ma* is *protestio y bore 'ma* run together, the time phrase after the verb-noun.
- *39 wythnos* is spoken in full; keep the caption form as "also heard as".
- Possible new section on Public services: *Staff ysgol* (school staff), covering pay, terms and holidays.
