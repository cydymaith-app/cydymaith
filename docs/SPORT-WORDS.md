# Sport words: collection

Words and phrases heard in Welsh sport broadcasts, collected per upload before the Sport pages are filled (phase 3 in `docs/TODO.md`). The same method as `FORECAST-WORDS.md`: record what was heard, then give each phrase one home.

Sources to watch: *Sgorio* (S4C, football), S4C's rugby coverage, and Radio Cymru commentary. Record phrases only, never a transcript. Every Welsh word stays a draft until a fluent speaker has checked it (`REVIEW.md`).
